Kenya

Kenya is a country in East Africa with a coastline on the Indian Ocean. It encompasses savannah, lakelands, the dramatic Great Rift Valley and mountain highlands. Its capital, Nairobi, is home to national parks and safari destinations. Kenya is known for its wildlife safaris, with many national parks and reserves that offer opportunities to see the "Big Five" animals: lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo, and rhinos.
